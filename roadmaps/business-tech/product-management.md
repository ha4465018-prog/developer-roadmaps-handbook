## 🛠️ Essential Skills & Tooling

Mastery of these foundational and specialized skills is required for competitive roles:

- **Product Strategy**
- **PRD Writing**
- **A/B Testing & Mixpanel**
- **User Research**
- **Agile Product Backlog**

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Learning Progression

### 🟢 Phase 1: Foundations & Core Principles (Beginner)
Focus on core fundamentals, language fluency, and core computer science prerequisites:

- [ ] Product Life Cycle & Market Needs Analysis
- [ ] Writing Feature Specs & PRDs
- [ ] Product Analytics Tools (Mixpanel / Amplitude / GA4)

### 🟡 Phase 2: Applied Engineering & Systems (Intermediate)
Build production architectures, understand frameworks, and work with data pipelines and systems:

- [ ] Product Roadmapping & Prioritization Frameworks (RICE, Kano)
- [ ] Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy
- [ ] PSPO I Certification

### 🔴 Phase 3: Advanced Architectures & Production Mastery (Advanced)
Scale systems, solve complex edge cases, optimize performance, and achieve industry leadership:

- [ ] VP of Product / Chief Product Officer (CPO) Strategy

## 🚀 Recommended Portfolio Projects

### 1. Production-Grade Product Management Capstone
Build an end-to-end, tested, and documented project demonstrating core principles of Product Management using Product Strategy, PRD Writing, A/B Testing & Mixpanel.

### 2. Real-World Benchmarking & Optimization Engine
Create an open-source tool or benchmark suite analyzing performance bottlenecks, throughput, or security compliance in product management workflows.

### 3. Industry Case Study & Prototype
Solve a real-world scenario (e.g., enterprise scalability, fault tolerance, or automation) with automated CI/CD pipelines, documentation, and Dockerized environments.
